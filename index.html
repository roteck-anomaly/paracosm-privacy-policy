Paracosm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Paracosm is an Android application for adults that allows users to create fictional worlds, characters, locations, and stories, and interact with them through AI-powered text-based roleplay.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Paracosm accesses, how that information is processed, and when it is transmitted to external services. Use of Paracosm is also governed by the Terms of Service.

2. Locally Stored Information

Paracosm primarily stores user-created content locally on the user's device. This includes fictional worlds, character profiles, locations, conversations, application settings, and character portraits.

API keys entered by the user are stored in encrypted local storage backed by Android's security facilities, and can additionally be protected by the device's lock screen.

Backups created by Paracosm are saved where the user chooses. Each backup includes a one-way (SHA-256) hash of the device's Android ID, so that a restore can recognize whether a backup came from the same device. The Android ID itself is never stored in a backup.

This information remains on the device unless a feature requiring external processing, synchronization, or sharing is used.

3. AI Processing

Paracosm uses AI models from two providers to generate narrative responses, character dialogue, story summaries, and other fictional content: Google's Gemini API and Anthropic's Claude API. The user chooses which provider and model each Paracosm uses.

To provide these features, Paracosm transmits relevant information directly from the device to the chosen provider, which may include:

  • User-written messages and conversation history.
  • Character descriptions and fictional world information.
  • Simulation instructions, settings, and story context.
  • Other user-created content included in AI requests.

Requests are made with the user's own API key for that provider. While Paracosm is in testing, requests to Google may instead be made with an API key provided by the developer. The developer does not receive or store the content of these requests.

Providers may temporarily cache request content to support processing and performance. Google and Anthropic may also retain or process submitted information according to their applicable service terms and policies.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in content they do not wish to transmit to an external AI provider.

4. Character Portrait Generation

Paracosm supports optional AI-generated character portraits. Character descriptions and, when the user chooses to use one, the character's current portrait as a reference image are transmitted to Google's Gemini services to generate portraits.

When a user uploads a photo as a character's portrait, Paracosm asks whether it is a photo of a real person. If the user answers yes, Paracosm stores a small marker beside the portrait on the device, and every portrait generated from that photo is kept modest. The marker is included when the character is backed up, synchronized, or shared. It is never sent to an AI provider.

5. Google Drive Synchronization

Paracosm offers optional synchronization through Google Drive. When enabled, Paracosm accesses the selected Google account and stores synchronized application files in Google Drive's application-specific storage.

Synchronized content may include saved fictional worlds, characters, conversations, portraits, and application settings. The selected Google account email address is stored locally to identify the account used for synchronization.

Synchronization may operate automatically while enabled. Users can disconnect Google Drive Sync without losing access to Paracosm's local features.

6. Sharing and Exporting

Users can share or save Paracosms, characters, and locations as files. A shared file contains the selected content and, where applicable, its character portraits. Sharing only happens when the user chooses to share, and the file is sent through the app the user selects. The developer does not receive shared files.

7. Free Trial Verification

When Paracosm offers a free trial, it verifies the trial with the developer's backend, which runs on Google Firebase. To do so, Paracosm sends the device's Android ID to the backend, where it is immediately converted into a one-way (SHA-256) hash. Only the hash, together with the trial's start and end times, is stored, so that a trial cannot be restarted on the same device. The Android ID itself is never stored.

Requests to the backend are protected by Firebase App Check, which uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that they come from a genuine copy of Paracosm. Google processes device integrity information for this purpose according to its own policies.

8. Content Reports

Users can report AI-generated responses they find offensive or harmful from within Paracosm. A report is sent to the developer's backend on Google Firebase, protected by Firebase App Check, and contains:

  • The reason chosen for the report, and any note the user adds.
  • The text of the reported response.
  • The AI provider and model the Paracosm uses, and the app version.

Reports never include images, API keys, device identifiers, or account information. The developer uses reports to investigate problems and improve how Paracosm guides AI-generated content.

9. Diagnostic and Error Reports

Paracosm records technical diagnostic information locally to support troubleshooting. This may include application errors, runtime logs, and details about application interactions.

Diagnostic reports are not automatically sent to the developer. Users can voluntarily submit an encrypted error-report attachment through an email application.

Submitted reports may be retained by the developer for investigating errors, resolving bugs, and improving application reliability.

10. Data Security

Paracosm uses HTTPS connections when communicating with Google, Anthropic, and Firebase services. User-provided API keys are stored in encrypted local storage backed by Android's security facilities, and are sent only to the provider they belong to.

Error-report attachments are encrypted before being prepared for transmission.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free. External providers are responsible for their own infrastructure and data-handling practices.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data generally remains on the device until the user deletes it or removes the application's local data.

Files uploaded through Google Drive Sync may remain in the user's Google Drive after synchronization is disabled. Disconnecting the feature does not necessarily delete existing cloud copies.

Data transmitted to Google or Anthropic is subject to that provider's applicable retention and deletion practices.

Trial records are kept for as long as Paracosm offers trials, so that a trial cannot be reused. Content reports and diagnostic reports are kept for as long as they are useful for investigating problems.

Users may request deletion of reports they submitted, or of their device's trial record, by contacting the email address below. Additional information may be needed to identify the relevant record.

12. Children's Privacy

Paracosm is intended only for adults aged 18 and older, and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent information to the developer, contact the email address below and it will be deleted.

13. Third-Party Services

Paracosm integrates with Google's Gemini API, Anthropic's Claude API, Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Firebase, and Google Play services. These services operate under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy. Anthropic's privacy information is available at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Paracosm introduces new features, integrations, or data-handling practices. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
