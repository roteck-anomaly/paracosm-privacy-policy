Paracosm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 (revision 2)

1. Overview

Paracosm is an Android application for adults that allows users to create fictional worlds, characters, locations, and stories, and interact with them through AI-powered text-based roleplay.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Paracosm accesses, how that information is processed, and when it is transmitted to external services. Use of Paracosm is also governed by the Terms of Service.

2. Locally Stored Information

Paracosm primarily stores user-created content locally on the user's device. This includes fictional worlds, character profiles, locations, conversations, application settings, and character portraits.

API keys entered by the user are stored in encrypted local storage backed by Android's security facilities, and can additionally be protected by the device's lock screen.

Backups created by Paracosm are saved where the user chooses. Each backup includes a one-way (SHA-256) hash of the device's Android ID, so that a restore can recognize whether a backup came from the same device. The Android ID itself is never stored in a backup.

Paracosm also stores on the device whether it has been unlocked, the unlock purchase's token from Google Play, whether the device belongs to a supporter, and when its free trial ends, so that it can check these without a network connection.

This information remains on the device unless a feature requiring external processing, synchronization, or sharing is used.

3. AI Processing

Paracosm uses AI models to generate narrative responses, character dialogue, story summaries, and other fictional content. Every model is reached through Venice (venice.ai), an AI service that passes each request to the company that makes the chosen model, such as Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), or Moonshot AI (Kimi). The user chooses which model each Paracosm uses.

To provide these features, Paracosm transmits relevant information directly from the device to Venice, which may include:

  • User-written messages and conversation history.
  • Character descriptions and fictional world information.
  • Simulation instructions, settings, and story context.
  • Other user-created content included in AI requests.

Requests are made with the user's own Venice API key and are billed to the user's Venice account. They go straight from the device to Venice and never pass through the developer's backend. The developer does not receive or store the content of these requests.

Venice states that it does not store the content of requests or responses, and that it prohibits the companies that make its models from storing, retaining, or using that content beyond the time needed to return a response. Those companies do receive the content of each request in order to process it. Venice's handling of requests is described in its Privacy Policy.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in content they do not wish to transmit to an external AI service.

4. Character Portrait Generation

Paracosm supports optional AI-generated character portraits, which are generated through Venice with the user's own Venice API key. Character descriptions and, when the user chooses to use one, the character's current portrait as a reference image are transmitted to Venice, which passes them to the company that makes the chosen image model.

Some portraits are kept modest, for example when the user's Venice account does not have Venice's Pro plan, or when the character is not clearly an adult. For these, Paracosm first sends a copy of the character's description through Venice to an AI model that removes mature details. That copy is only used for the portrait and is never saved. Whether the user's Venice account has the Pro plan is a setting the user chooses, stored on the device.

When a user uploads a photo as a character's portrait, Paracosm asks whether it is a photo of a real person. If the user answers yes, Paracosm stores a small marker beside the portrait on the device, and every portrait generated from that photo is kept modest. The marker is included when the character is backed up, synchronized, or shared. It is never sent to an AI service.

5. Google Drive Synchronization

Paracosm offers optional synchronization through Google Drive. When enabled, Paracosm accesses the selected Google account and stores synchronized application files in Google Drive's application-specific storage.

Synchronized content may include saved fictional worlds, characters, conversations, portraits, and application settings. The selected Google account email address is stored locally to identify the account used for synchronization.

Synchronization may operate automatically while enabled. Users can disconnect Google Drive Sync without losing access to Paracosm's local features.

6. Sharing and Exporting

Users can share or save Paracosms, characters, and locations as files. A shared file contains the selected content and, where applicable, its character portraits. Sharing only happens when the user chooses to share, and the file is sent through the app the user selects. The developer does not receive shared files.

7. Free Trial and Unlock Purchase

Paracosm offers a free trial, which it verifies with the developer's backend, running on Google Firebase. To do so, Paracosm sends the device's Android ID to the backend, where it is immediately converted into a one-way (SHA-256) hash. Only the hash, together with the trial's start and end times, is stored, so that a trial cannot be restarted on the same device. The Android ID itself is never stored.

Requests to the backend are protected by Firebase App Check, which uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that they come from a genuine copy of Paracosm. Google processes device integrity information for this purpose according to its own policies.

Paracosm can be unlocked with a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Paracosm never receives payment details, and asks Google Play only whether the user's Google Play account owns the unlock.

8. Donations and Supporters

Users can donate to the developer through Google Play. Google Play processes each donation under its own terms and privacy policy, and Paracosm never receives payment details. Donating is optional and never unlocks anything.

To recognise supporters without any sign-in, Paracosm sends each donation to the developer's backend on Google Firebase, together with the device's Android ID and, once Paracosm is unlocked, the unlock purchase's token from Google Play. The backend confirms the donation with Google Play, then stores a supporter record containing one-way (SHA-256) hashes of those two identifiers and, for each donation, its Google Play order number, the donation product, the price Google Play showed when it was recorded, and the date. The identifiers themselves are never stored.

Supporter records let Paracosm show a Supporter badge, and let the developer recognise reports from supporters so they can be looked at first. To know whether to show the badge, Paracosm sends the same two identifiers to the backend once after it is installed, and again before an error report is sent. The backend uses them to look for a matching supporter record, and when it finds one, it adds any hash the record was missing, so a supporter is recognised on each of their devices.

9. Content Reports

Users can report AI-generated responses they find offensive or harmful from within Paracosm. A report is sent to the developer's backend on Google Firebase, protected by Firebase App Check, and contains:

  • The reason chosen for the report, and any note the user adds.
  • The text of the reported response.
  • The AI provider and model the Paracosm uses, and the app version.

Reports never include images, API keys, or account information. To mark reports that come from supporters, Paracosm also sends the device's Android ID and, once Paracosm is unlocked, the unlock purchase's token. The backend uses them only to check whether the sender is a supporter, as described in Donations and Supporters, and never stores them with the report; only whether the report came from a supporter is stored.

The developer uses reports to investigate problems and improve how Paracosm guides AI-generated content.

10. Diagnostic and Error Reports

Paracosm records technical diagnostic information locally to support troubleshooting. This may include application errors, runtime logs, and details about application interactions.

Diagnostic reports are not automatically sent to the developer. Users can voluntarily submit an encrypted error-report attachment through an email application.

Before an error report is sent, Paracosm asks the backend whether the device belongs to a supporter, so that supporters' reports can be marked and looked at first. The encrypted attachment includes the same one-way hashes of the device's Android ID and of the unlock purchase's token that supporter records use.

Submitted reports may be retained by the developer for investigating errors, resolving bugs, and improving application reliability.

11. Data Security

Paracosm uses HTTPS connections when communicating with Venice, Google, and Firebase services. The user's Venice API key is stored in encrypted local storage backed by Android's security facilities, and is sent only to Venice.

Error-report attachments are encrypted before being prepared for transmission.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk- free. External services are responsible for their own infrastructure and data-handling practices.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data generally remains on the device until the user deletes it or removes the application's local data.

Files uploaded through Google Drive Sync may remain in the user's Google Drive after synchronization is disabled. Disconnecting the feature does not necessarily delete existing cloud copies.

Data transmitted to Venice, and to the companies that make its models, is subject to their own retention and deletion practices.

Trial records are kept for as long as Paracosm offers trials, so that a trial cannot be reused. Supporter records are kept for as long as Paracosm recognises supporters. Content reports and diagnostic reports are kept for as long as they are useful for investigating problems.

Users may request deletion of reports they submitted, of their device's trial record, or of their supporter record, by contacting the email address below. Additional information may be needed to identify the relevant record.

13. Children's Privacy

Paracosm is intended only for adults aged 18 and older, and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent information to the developer, contact the email address below and it will be deleted.

14. Third-Party Services

Paracosm integrates with Venice, Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Firebase, Google Play services, and Google Play Billing. Through Venice, requests also reach the companies that make Venice's models. These services operate under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Venice's privacy information is available at venice.ai/legal/privacy-policy. Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
