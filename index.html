Paracosm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

Paracosm is an Android application that allows users to create fictional worlds, characters, locations, and stories, and interact with them through AI-powered text-based roleplay.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Paracosm accesses, how that information is processed, and when it is transmitted to external services.

2. Locally Stored Information

Paracosm primarily stores user-created content locally on the user's device. This includes fictional worlds, character profiles, locations, conversations, application settings, and generated character portraits.

This information remains on the device unless a feature requiring external processing or synchronization is used.

3. AI Processing

Paracosm uses Google's Gemini API to generate narrative responses, character dialogue, and other fictional content.

To provide these features, Paracosm transmits relevant information to Google, which may include:

  • User-written messages and conversation history.
  • Character descriptions and fictional world information.
  • Simulation instructions, settings, and story context.
  • Other user-created content included in AI requests.

Gemini may temporarily cache request content to support processing and performance. Google may also retain or process submitted information according to its applicable service terms and policies.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in content they do not wish to transmit to an external AI provider.

4. Character Portrait Generation

Paracosm supports optional AI-generated character portraits. Character descriptions and, when provided, reference images may be transmitted to Google's Gemini services to generate portraits.

Reference images are only submitted when the user chooses to use them for portrait generation.

5. Google Drive Synchronization

Paracosm offers optional synchronization through Google Drive. When enabled, Paracosm accesses the selected Google account and stores synchronized application files in Google Drive's application-specific storage.

Synchronized content may include saved fictional worlds, characters, conversations, portraits, and application settings. The selected Google account email address is stored locally to identify the account used for synchronization.

Synchronization may operate automatically while enabled. Users can disconnect Google Drive Sync without losing access to Paracosm's local features.

6. Diagnostic and Error Reports

Paracosm records technical diagnostic information locally to support troubleshooting. This may include application errors, runtime logs, and details about application interactions.

Diagnostic reports are not automatically emailed to the developer. Users can voluntarily submit an encrypted error-report attachment through an email application.

Submitted reports may be retained by the developer for investigating errors, resolving bugs, and improving application reliability.

7. Data Security

Paracosm uses HTTPS connections when communicating with the supported Google APIs. User-provided API credentials are stored in encrypted local storage backed by Android's security facilities.

Error-report attachments are encrypted before being prepared for transmission.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free. External providers are responsible for their own infrastructure and data-handling practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data generally remains on the device until the user deletes it or removes the application's local data.

Files uploaded through Google Drive Sync may remain in the user's Google Drive after synchronization is disabled. Disconnecting the feature does not necessarily delete existing cloud copies.

Data transmitted to Google services is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices.

Users may request deletion of diagnostic reports previously submitted to the developer by contacting the email address below. Additional information may be needed to identify the relevant report.

9. Third-Party Services

Paracosm integrates with Google's Gemini API, Google Sign-In, and Google Drive. These services operate under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy .

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Paracosm introduces new features, integrations, or data-handling practices. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
